Left Step 1: Old Ceiling Trim Removal & Flat 2x4 Leveling Sleepers (Flush Baseboard Stays)

Zero Baseboard Demo
  • Leave the Bottom Baseboard In Place: Because your back wall baseboard is flush with the wall surface, do not remove it. Your sleepers and cabinet boxes push directly against it.
  • Pry Off Old Top Ceiling Moulding: Use a flat pry bar and putty knife to remove the existing ceiling trim across the left alcove (and across the TV wall and right alcove) so your new 1x4 header and crown sit flat.
  • Mark Wall Studs with Tape: Locate studs on the back wall and mark them with blue tape at 32" and 85" high.
  • Lay Two 52.5" 2x4s Flat (1.5" Tall): Lay two 52.5" 2x4s flat on the oak floor (rear 2x4 against the flush back baseboard; front 2x4 at 16.5" from the back wall). Shim dead level left-to-right and front-to-back, then screw into the subfloor/wall plate with #8 × 3" screws.

Left Step 2: Building & Joining the Two Separate 26.5" W × 28.0" H Plywood Boxes

3/4" Kreg Pocket Joinery
  • Assemble Two Identical 26.5" W Boxes: Using your 16.5" wide plywood strips, pocket-screw a 25.0" W × 16.5" D Bottom Deck and two 25.0" W × 4.0" D Top Stretchers between two 28.0" H × 16.5" D side panels (`0.75" + 25.0" + 0.75" = 26.5" W`).
  • Bore 2" Xbox HDMI & Vent Holes in Box #2: Before lifting Box #2 onto the sleepers, drill two 2.0" holes through its upper back/right side so HDMI cords pass invisibly to the TV.
  • Fuse Both Boxes on the Leveled 2x4s: Set Box #1 and Box #2 on the flat 2x4s (`26.5" + 26.5" = 53.0" W`). Clamp their middle touching walls together and drive four 1-1/4" screws through the center walls, then drive #8 × 3" screws through the rear cleats into the wall studs.

Left Step 3: Nailing the 1x2 Face Frame Grid (Creates Two 24.5" × 25.0" Openings)

Hides Center Seam
  • Top & Bottom 1x2 Rails (`53.5" W`): Cut two 1x2 boards (`1.5"` tall) to the full 53.5" alcove width. Glue and brad-nail the Top Rail across the top of the boxes, and the Bottom Rail across the bottom deck.
  • Three Vertical 1x2 Stiles (`25.0" H`): Cut three 1x2 stiles to 25.0" H. Nail the Center Stile directly over the two touching center walls (`0.75" + 0.75" = 1.5"`—it covers the seam completely!). Nail the Left and Right stiles against the left wall and fireplace brick. *(Note: If your left side-wall baseboard sticks out slightly, snip a tiny notch at the bottom back corner of the Left stile).*
  • 1x2 Toe-Kick Trim (`53.5" W`): Nail one 53.5" strip of 1x2 across the front of the flat 2x4 sleeper.

Left Step 4: Two-Pass 53.5" × 18.0" Butcher Block Rip

1.5" Hevea

Clamp your straight-edge onto the upside-down 25" butcher block slab. Set circular saw depth to 3/4" for Pass #1, then 1-5/8" for Pass #2 to rip cleanly to 18.0" D, and crosscut to 53.5" W. Fasten from underneath through 3/8" oversized holes in the top stretchers.

Left Step 5: 55.0" Upper 1x12 Bookcase & Face Frame

86.0" Top Height

Pocket-screw eight 25-3/8" shelves between three 55.0" vertical 1x12 pillars (`13.0"` spacing). Lift onto the rear of the butcher block, anchor a top cleat under the top shelf into wall studs, and brad-nail 1x2 face frames over the front edges.

Rattan Door Step 1: Double-Door Math & 1x3 Frame Assembly (12.75" W × 26.0" H)

Build 4 Identical Doors
  • Why Each Door Is 12.75" W × 26.0" H: Each base box opening is 24.5" W × 25.0" H. Adding a 1/2" overlay on all four outer edges equals 25.5" W × 26.0" H per pair. Divided by 2 doors = 12.75" W × 26.0" H per door.
  • 1x3 Cut Formula (`2.5"` actual width):
    • Cut 8 Vertical Stiles (2 per door) to 26.0" Long.
    • Cut 8 Horizontal Rails (2 per door) to 7.75" Long (`2.5" stile + 7.75" rail + 2.5" stile = 12.75" Total Width`).
  • Kreg Pocket-Hole Joinery: Set your Kreg jig to 3/4". Drill 2 pocket holes on the back face at each end of the 7.75" rails. Glue and drive 1-1/4" coarse pocket screws into the 26.0" stiles on a flat workbench.

Rattan Door Step 2: Routing the 3/8" × 3/8" Rear Rabbet Groove & Sandwiching the Cane

Bearing-Guided Router Cut
  • 1. Chuck a 3/8" Rabbeting Bit (With Pilot Bearing): Set the router cutting depth to 3/8" (0.375")—exactly half the thickness of your 3/4" door frame.
  • 2. Route Around the Inside Back Window: Lay the assembled door frame back-side up. Run the router clockwise around the inside perimeter. The bearing rides along the wood, carving a clean 3/8" W × 3/8" D L-shaped step.
  • 3. Paint the Wood Frame NOW: Prime and paint your 4 routed door frames in Swedish Blue before installing the rattan so you never splatter blue paint onto the natural cane.
  • 4. Soak, Staple & Add the 1/4" × 3/8" Trim Strip: Soak the 8.5" × 21.75" rattan panel in warm water for 35 minutes. Staple it into the routed step with 3/8" T50 staples, then glue and brad-nail a 1/4" × 3/8" pine lattice strip into the step over the staples.

Rattan Door Step 3: Boring the Eight 35mm Blum Hinge Cups & Hanging the 4 Doors

Kreg 35mm Hinge Jig
  • Cup Hole Placement (`3.5"` From Top & Bottom): Clamp your Kreg 35mm Concealed Hinge Jig onto the back of the outer 2.5" stile centered at 3.5" down from the top and 3.5" up from the bottom (this clears the rail pocket screws completely).
  • Bore & Snap In Blum 38N Hinges: Drill to the pre-set depth collar (`1/2"` deep), press the Blum Compact `1/2" overlay` hinge into the 35mm cup, and drive the two small mounting screws in.
  • Hang on the 1x2 Face Frame: Wrap the C-shaped wing of each hinge around the inside lip of your 1x2 cabinet stiles, drive the single face-frame screw in, and use the 3-way cam screws to align a uniform `1/16"` center reveal between each pair of doors.
