Rattan Door Step 1: Exact Double-Door Sizing & 1x3 Pocket-Hole Frame Build 4 Identical Doors: 12.75" W x 26.0" H

Why 12.75" Wide x 26.0" High is the exact mathematical size when putting two doors on each box:

  • The Opening Width Math: Each of your two base boxes has a 24.5" Wide opening inside the 1x2 face frame. With a 1/2" hinge overlay on the left stile (+0.5") and right stile (+0.5"), the pair of doors must span 24.5" + 1.0" = 25.5" Total Width. Divided by 2 doors = 12.75" Wide per door.
  • The 1x3 Cut Math: A 1x3 select pine or poplar board is actually 2.5" wide x 0.75" thick.
    • Cut 2 Vertical Stiles to 26.0" Long.
    • Cut 2 Horizontal Rails to 7.75" Long (2.5" + 7.75" + 2.5" = 12.75" W).
  • Pocket-Hole Assembly: Set your Kreg jig to 3/4". Drill 2 pocket holes on the back face at each end of the 7.75" rails. Glue the ends and clamp/screw the rails between the 26.0" stiles on a flat surface.
Rattan Door Step 2: How to Route the "Stepped Rabbet Groove" on the Back 3/8" Wide x 3/8" Deep Stepped Groove
  • 1. Chuck a 3/8" Rabbeting Bit into Your Router: The spinning pilot bearing rides along the inside window of your assembled door frame, cutting a uniform 3/8" wide step with zero fence required.
  • 2. Set Bit Depth to 3/8" (Half of the 3/4" Board): Leaves a strong 3/8" front lip and carves a recessed 3/8" shelf on the back.
  • 3. Route Clockwise Around the Back Window: Lay the assembled door back-side up and route around the inside perimeter.
  • 4. Paint the Wood Frame BEFORE Stapling Rattan: Prime and paint your 4 routed wood frames in Swedish Blue first so the natural rattan stays spotless.
Rattan Door Step 3: Soaking, Stapling & Drilling the 35mm Hinge Cups Drum-Tight Shrink Fit
  • 1. Cut & Soak Rattan in Warm Water (35 Mins): Cut 4 pieces of rattan webbing to 8.5" Wide x 21.75" High and soak in warm water for 35 minutes so the cane fibers expand and soften.
  • 2. Staple Opposite Sides Under Tension: Staple Top edge first with 3/8" T50 staples, pull taut and staple Bottom edge, then alternate Left and Right sides.
  • 3. Glue & Pin the 1/4" Backing Strips: Glue and brad-nail your 1/4" x 3/8" pine lattice strips into the groove over the staples. As the cane dries, it shrinks drum-tight.
  • 4. Drill the Two 35mm Hinge Cups: Clamp your Kreg Concealed Hinge Jig onto the back of the outer 2.5" stile at 3.5" from the top and bottom, drill the 35mm cups, and screw in your Blum hinges.
